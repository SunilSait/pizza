Our Story

From a Tiny Oven
to Chennai's Heart

In 2008, Ravi Krishnamurthy — a passionate home cook with a dream and a secondhand wood-fired oven — opened Slice & Dice in a 12-seat room on Marina Road. He had no prior restaurant experience. What he had was an obsession with perfect pizza.

"I didn't want to make Indian pizza. I wanted to make pizza — properly — in India. There's a difference."
— Ravi Krishnamurthy, Founder

Word spread fast. Within six months, the queue outside stretched down the street. Today, Slice & Dice serves 500+ customers daily — but every pizza is still made with the same care as that very first Margherita in 2008.

Our Journey

The Road to Flavour

2008
The First Slice
Ravi opens a 12-seat pizza spot on Marina Road with a secondhand wood-fired oven and ₹80,000 savings.
2011
Delivery Launched
Slice & Dice expands to home delivery with 2 motorbikes. 30-minute guarantee introduced — and kept ever since.
2015
First Major Award
Wins "Best Independent Pizzeria" at the Chennai Food Awards. The BBQ Chicken pizza named dish of the year.
2019
Full Renovation
Slice & Dice expands to a 60-seat restaurant with a redesigned kitchen, new stone oven, and a dedicated catering wing.
2023
500,000 Pizzas Served
We reach a half-million pizzas milestone. 15k+ loyal customers. Slice Rewards loyalty program launched.
What We Stand For

Our Beliefs

🌿

Always Fresh

No freezers. No preservatives. Every ingredient is sourced daily and prepared from scratch each morning.

🔥

Craft Over Convenience

Our dough ferments 72 hours. Our oven runs at 450°C. We take the slow path because it produces the best results.

🤝

Community First

We buy locally, hire locally, and give back locally. Our suppliers are Chennai-based farmers and small businesses.

♻️

Zero Waste Kitchen

We compost, recycle, and minimise single-use plastics. Our packaging is 90% plant-based or recyclable.
